GATK SmithWatermanJavaAligner parity (JAVA implementation path).

§Layout

Backtrack is a flat row-major Vec<i32>. Scores use two rolling rows plus a saved last-column vector: CIGAR only needs scores on the final row/column to pick the end cell, then follows btrack.

§Production HC sizes

Typical SoftClip realign is ~read×hap (≈100–250). Hap-to-ref Indel uses SW_PAD (±10 N) around haplotypes ≈50–300. Full score matrices are not retained.
