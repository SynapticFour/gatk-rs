Operational safety ceiling on reads retained in one assembly region after positional DS.

GATK 4.4 caps per alignment start (default 50), not total depth. Staggered starts (e.g. centromere / P12 full-30×) can leave tens of thousands of reads per region; finalize + shard Arc residency then Peak-RSS OOMs a 16 GiB host (measured ~3 GiB spikes on NA12878 20:10000000-10100000). Cap is fail-closed Peak-RSS hygiene — not a genotype-contract downsampler. Was 100 000; tightened after 100 kb overnight jetsam.
